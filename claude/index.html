Get Claude Ultra for 6 months, free

Here's everything from the reel. Anthropic runs a program called Claude for Open Source. If you help keep open source running, they give you 6 months of Claude Ultra, their top plan, at no cost. Their own words: "Thank you for everything you ship." It isn't automatic, you fill in a short application. If you maintain or contribute to real projects, it's worth the five minutes.

What you need

  • A Claude account. If you already have one, apply with the same email so it lands on that account.
  • Open-source work that fits one of these. You maintain packages others build on (500+ dependent repos, 100+ dependent packages, or 200,000+ combined monthly downloads on npm, PyPI, crates.io, RubyGems or similar).
  • Or you're a listed committer or maintainer on a recognised foundation or language project, like CPython, the Rust team, Node.js TSC, Apache PMC, CNCF, Kubernetes, the Linux kernel, Django or Rails.
  • Or you've authored 100+ pull requests merged into repos you don't own in the last 12 months.
  • Or one of your repos had 20+ outside contributors with merged PRs in the last 12 months, or has an OpenSSF criticality score of 0.4 or higher.
  • Cost: nothing for 6 months.

Set it up

  1. Open the official page: claude.com/contact-sales/claude-for-oss.
  2. Read the Who should apply list and find the path that fits you.
  3. Fill in the form with your name and email (use your existing Claude email if you have an account) and the projects you work on. Links to your repos or profile help.
  4. Don't quite fit? Anthropic says to apply anyway and tell them what you maintain.
  5. Wait for their reply. If you're approved, Ultra is added to your account.

Use it

  1. Claude Code: install it, run claude inside a project folder, and let it read, edit and test your code.
  2. Cowork: use it in the Claude desktop app to hand off multi-step work on your own files.
  3. Models: switch between Fable, Opus, Sonnet and Haiku in the model picker.

What you get

Claude Ultra

Anthropic's top individual plan, with the highest usage limits.

Claude Code

The coding agent that works right in your terminal and repo.

Cowork

Claude doing longer tasks for you on your desktop.

Every model

Fable, Opus, Sonnet and Haiku, all in one plan.

Good to know

  • It's an application, not a coupon. Not everyone who applies gets it.
  • It's for people who maintain or contribute to open source. A personal repo with a few stars won't qualify on its own.
  • It lasts 6 months. Read the program terms for what happens to your plan after that.
  • Usage limits are very high, not literally unlimited.
